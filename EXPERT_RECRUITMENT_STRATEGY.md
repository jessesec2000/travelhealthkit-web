# Expert Panel Recruitment Strategy

## Where to Find Experts

### Medical Experts

**Infectious Disease / Travel Medicine:**
- **ASTMH** (American Society of Tropical Medicine & Hygiene) - https://www.astmh.org/
  - Contact: education@astmh.org
  - Ask for referrals to travel medicine specialists
- **IMVC** (International Medical Vaccine Clinics) - Find local clinics, contact lead physicians
- **University Hospital Travel Clinics** - Johns Hopkins, Mayo Clinic, Mass General have dedicated travel medicine departments
- **LinkedIn Search:** "Travel Medicine" + "Infectious Disease" + location

**Emergency Medicine:**
- **ACEP** (American College of Emergency Physicians) - https://www.acep.org/
  - Wilderness Medicine Section
- **SAEM** (Society for Academic Emergency Medicine)
- **Reddit:** r/emergencymedicine (post asking for volunteers)

**Pediatric Travel Medicine:**
- **AAP** (American Academy of Pediatrics) - Section on International Child Health
- **IPOW** (International Pediatric Outreach Workgroup)

### Adventure/Outdoor Experts

**Remote Travel / Expedition Leaders:**
- **The Explorers Club** - https://www.explorers.org/ (NYC-based, global network)
- **National Geographic Explorers** - Apply to contact explorers: explorers@natgeo.com
- **Royal Geographical Society** - https://www.rgs.org/ (UK-based)
- **Outward Bound** instructors - contact regional directors
- **NOLS** (National Outdoor Leadership School) instructors

**Extreme Sports:**
- **USPA** (United States Parachute Association) - for BASE/skydiving experts
- **American Alpine Club** - for climbing/mountaineering experts
- **PADI** / **NAUI** - for diving experts
- **Surfrider Foundation** - for water sports experts
- **International Mountain Guides Association**

**Family Travel:**
- **Family Travel Association** - https://familytravel.org/
- **Travel + Leisure Family** editors
- **Parents Magazine** travel section
- **Mom bloggers** with medical/health focus (FeverBee, Scary Mommy networks)

## Recruitment Approaches

### Option 1: Formal Advisory Board
**Compensation:** $500-1000 per review cycle
**Commitment:** Quarterly reviews
**Benefits:** Advisory board title, app credits, networking

### Option 2: Volunteer Expert Review
**Incentive:** Free lifetime premium app access
**Recognition:** Listed as "Expert Contributor" in app
**Low commitment:** One-time review

### Option 3: Academic Partnership
**Target:** Medical residents, nursing students, public health students
**Benefit:** Resume building, academic credit (if arranged with university)
**Platform:** Reach out to program directors

## Outreach Templates by Channel

[Your name]"

## Screening Questions for Experts

Before accepting an expert, ask:

1. **Credentials:** "Can you share your professional background relevant to [area]?"
2. **Experience:** "Have you worked with travelers/adventure sports participants?"
3. **Conflict Check:** "Do you have any competing products or financial conflicts?"
4. **Time:** "Can you complete review by [date]?"
5. **Compensation:** "Our budget is [X] - does this work for you?"

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Anyone asking for equity without proven contribution
- "Experts" with no verifiable credentials
- People who want payment upfront without work
- Those pushing their own products/services
- Anyone who won't sign a content review agreement

## Agreement Template

Create a simple 1-page agreement covering:
- Confidentiality of app content pre-launch
- No compensation for defamatory feedback
- Right to use their name as "Expert Reviewer" (optional)
- Acknowledgment this is advisory, not medical endorsement

## Timeline

**Week 1:** Post to professional orgs, LinkedIn outreach
**Week 2-3:** Screen responses, conduct interviews
**Week 4:** Select panel, send review materials
**Week 6-8:** Collect feedback
**Week 9:** Implement critical fixes

## Budget Estimate

- 6 experts × $500 honorarium = $3,000
- OR 6 experts × lifetime free access = $0 + marketing value
- Professional org posting fees: $0-500
- Total: $0-3,500

## Alternative: AI-Assisted Expert Review

If human experts are slow/unavailable, consider:
1. Review content with medical AI (Claude, GPT-4)
2. Flag specific claims for expert verification
3. Present experts with AI-flagged items only (saves their time)

This hybrid approach can reduce expert time commitment by 70%.
