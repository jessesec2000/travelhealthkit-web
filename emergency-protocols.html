Emergency Protocols

Step-by-step guides for travel emergencies

🆘 In a life-threatening emergency?
Call local emergency services immediately
112 / 911
😰
Anaphylaxis (Severe Allergic Reaction)
Difficulty breathing, throat swelling
Critical
1
Use EpiPen immediately - Inject into outer thigh (through clothing OK)
2
Call emergency services - Even if symptoms improve
3
Lie flat with legs elevated (if breathing OK) or sit if breathing difficult
4
Use second EpiPen after 5-15 minutes if symptoms persist
Do NOT:
Wait to see if it gets better
Give antihistamines first (too slow)
Let person walk around
🩸
Severe Bleeding
Blood spurting, soaking through bandages
Critical
1
Apply direct pressure with clean cloth - maintain for 10 minutes minimum
2
If bleeding through, add more layers - do NOT remove original dressing
3
Apply tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound if limb bleeding (note time)
4
Call emergency services immediately
Do NOT:
Remove embedded objects
Apply tourniquet to neck/chest/abdomen
Check wound frequently (maintain pressure)
💔
Chest Pain / Heart Attack
Crushing pressure, pain to arm/jaw
Critical
1
Call emergency services immediately
2
Stop activity, sit or lie down
3
Chew (don't swallow) 325mg aspirin if available
4
Take nitroglycerin if prescribed
5
Loosen tight clothing, stay calm
🏔️
Altitude Sickness (Severe)
Confusion, difficulty walking, breathlessness at rest
Urgent
1
Stop ascent immediately
2
Descend immediately if symptoms worsen - do not wait
3
Give oxygen if available
4
Dexamethasone if prescribed for emergency use
5
Evacuate if confusion, difficulty walking, or breathing problems
Do NOT:
Continue ascent with symptoms
Leave person alone
Wait to see if it improves
🌡️
Heat Stroke
Body temp >104°F, confusion, hot skin
Critical
1
Call emergency services - This is life-threatening
2
Move to shade/air conditioning immediately
3
Remove excess clothing
4
Cool rapidly: ice packs at neck/armpits/groin, cold water immersion if possible
Do NOT:
Give fever-reducing medications (acetaminophen/ibuprofen)
Give fluids if confused (choking risk)
🚨 Call Emergency Services
