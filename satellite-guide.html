🛰️ How They Work

Understanding satellite communication helps you use your device effectively in the field.

1
Satellite Constellation
These devices connect to satellites in low Earth orbit (Iridium network) or geostationary satellites. The Iridium network uses 66 satellites for global coverage including poles.
2
Message Transmission
Your device sends signals to the nearest satellite, which relays it to ground stations. Messages typically send within seconds to minutes, depending on satellite visibility.
3
Clear View Required
Satellites are in space—your device needs a clear view of the sky. Dense forest canopy, deep canyons, or buildings can block signals and delay transmission.
4
Global Coverage
Unlike cell phones that need towers, satellite communicators work anywhere on Earth. This includes oceans, polar regions, deserts, and remote wilderness areas.
🆘 What Happens When You Press SOS?
You Press the SOS Button
Most devices require holding the button for 3-5 seconds to prevent accidental activation.
Emergency Signal Sent
Your device transmits your GPS location and emergency alert to the satellite network.
24/7 Monitoring Center
Alert goes to a global emergency response coordination center (GEOS Alliance for most devices).
Local Authorities Contacted
The monitoring center contacts the appropriate rescue service for your location (park rangers, coast guard, etc.).
Two-Way Communication
If your device supports it, rescuers can message you for details: nature of emergency, number of people, injuries, etc.
Help is Dispatched
Search and rescue teams are deployed to your GPS coordinates. Stay put if possible.

⚠️ Important Limitations

🚨 Not 100% Reliable
Satellite communication can fail. Dense tree cover, steep canyons, severe weather, or device malfunction can prevent signal transmission. Always have a backup plan and tell someone your itinerary.
🔋 Battery Life
Most devices last 3-14 days depending on use. Cold weather drastically reduces battery life. Carry backup power banks and know your device's power-saving modes.
⏱️ Response Time
Rescue can take hours or days depending on location, weather, and available resources. Your device calls for help—it doesn't guarantee immediate rescue.
💸 Subscription Required
Most devices require an active subscription to function. If you cancel, you lose messaging capabilities. The ACR ResQLink is the exception—no fees, but no messaging either.
🎯 Accuracy Limits
GPS accuracy varies. In emergencies, stay visible and use signaling devices (whistle, mirror, bright clothing) to help rescuers locate you once they're in the area.
