Everything you need to prepare for your travel medicine appointment

Vaccination Timeline
8+ Weeks Before
Schedule your appointment
Some vaccines require multiple doses spaced weeks apart
6 Weeks Before
Start Hepatitis B series
3-dose series: Day 0, 1 month, 6 months
4 Weeks Before
Get Japanese Encephalitis
2-dose series: Day 0 and Day 28
2 Weeks Before
Final vaccine doses
Most vaccines need 2 weeks to reach full effectiveness
Last Minute (Not Ideal)
Some protection is better than none
Discuss accelerated schedules with your doctor
Discussion Checklist
📋 Topics to Cover
  • Review your destination(s) and itinerary
  • Discuss planned activities (hiking, diving, etc.)
  • Review current medications and allergies
  • Get required & recommended vaccinations
  • Obtain malaria prophylaxis prescription if needed
  • Get traveler's diarrhea antibiotics (just in case)
  • Review altitude sickness prevention (if applicable)
  • Discuss jet lag management strategies
Questions to Ask
"Which vaccines are required vs. recommended for my destination?"
"How do my current medications interact with travel medications?"
"What should I do if I get sick while traveling?"
"Do I need proof of vaccination (Yellow Card) for entry?"
"Are there any current disease outbreaks I should know about?"
"What's the best insect repellent for this region?"
