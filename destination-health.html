Current Disease Risks
🦟
Malaria
Moderate Risk

Risk in Terai region (south) below 1,500m, especially during monsoon (June-Sept). No risk in Kathmandu or mountain areas.

🦟
Dengue Fever
High Risk

Increasing cases in Terai region and Kathmandu Valley. Day-biting mosquitoes. No vaccine available—prevention is key.

⛰️
Altitude Sickness (AMS)
High Risk

Risk above 2,500m. Symptoms: headache, nausea, fatigue. Can progress to life-threatening HACE/HAPE. Ascend slowly!

💧
Traveler's Diarrhea
Moderate Risk

Common from contaminated food/water. Carry antibiotics (azithromycin) and oral rehydration salts.

🧊
Japanese Encephalitis
Low Risk

Rare but serious. Risk in rural farming areas during monsoon. Consider vaccine for extended rural stays.

Water & Food Safety
💧

Drink Only Safe Water

Bottled, boiled, or purified water only. Use purification tablets or filters for trekking. Avoid ice cubes.

🍽️

Eat Cooked Foods

"Boil it, cook it, peel it, or forget it." Avoid raw vegetables, salads, and unpeeled fruits.

🥛

Avoid Dairy

Unpasteurized milk and dairy products are risky. Only consume if from trusted sources.

🌶️

Spicy Food Caution

Nepali food can be very spicy. Try mild versions first if you have a sensitive stomach.

🍵

Dal Bhat Power

The traditional meal (rice, lentils, vegetables) is generally safe and provides good energy for trekking.

Altitude Guidelines
⛰️ Trekking Safely
📈

Golden Rule

Above 3,000m: Sleep no more than 300-500m higher per day. Include acclimatization days every 3-4 days.

🌿

Diamox (Acetazolamide)

Can help prevent AMS. Discuss with your doctor before the trip. Start 1-2 days before ascent.

⚠️

Descend Immediately If

Severe headache, confusion, difficulty walking, or shortness of breath at rest. AMS can be fatal.
